I've spent 2023 with more headsets on my face than is medically advisable, and if you'd asked me in January what the year would deliver, I'd have guessed a quiet contraction. I'd have been wrong about the quiet part. Sony dropped the PSVR2 on February 22 at $549.99 — dual OLED panels, eye tracking, headset haptics — and Meta answered with the Quest 3 on October 10 at $499.99, pancake lenses and full-color passthrough. Two new headsets in one year is the kind of hardware cadence this medium hadn't seen since the original boom.

But hardware was never the story. The games were. We got a free VR mode for Resident Evil Village that turns a flat horror sequel into one of the best VR experiences on any platform, a ground-up Assassin's Creed built for Quest, and a mod scene that dragged Crysis, Portal 2, and Far Cry into room-scale reality. Some of these are native. Some are mods. All of them are playable in VR as of right now, which is the only test that matters at year's end. Here are the 35 that earned a spot.

## #35: No More Rainbows

![No More Rainbows](/images/games/no-more-rainbows-vr-hero.jpg)

<div class="rank-meta">2023 · Native VR</div>

No More Rainbows is the one that made me put down the controller and trust my hands. It's a first-person platformer with no sticks at all — you swing, vault, and claw through four worlds using nothing but tracked motion, Gorilla Tag's locomotion philosophy with a level designer behind it. If you came to VR for the feeling of your body being in the game, this is the purest argument on the list.

## #34: Demeo Battles

![Demeo Battles](/images/games/demeo-battles-vr-hero.jpg)

<div class="rank-meta">2023 · Native VR</div>

Demeo Battles takes the tabletop RPG VR fans already loved and turns it competitive — you sit across the board from your friends, figurines in hand, and try to out-think them. It's the same diorama charm as Demeo, pointed at winning instead of surviving.

[Read the full Demeo Battles review](/games/demeo-battles)

## #33: Ghosts of Tabor

![Ghosts of Tabor](/images/games/ghosts-of-tabor-vr-hero.jpg)

<div class="rank-meta">2023 · Native VR</div>

Ghosts of Tabor is the extraction shooter VR needed and the one I'm most nervous recommending. It drags Tarkov's loot-or-lose tension into your living room with two real hands on the gun, and the stakes feel real because they are — but it launched rough and stayed that way in places. For the right player, no other VR game delivers this much paranoia.

## The Year in One Breath

If you'd told me in January that 2023 would close with a free RE Village VR Mode at the top, a standalone Asgard's Wrath 2 at number five, and a modded Crysis I could actually swing a fist in, I'd have called you optimistic. The hardware shipped, the modders showed up, and the studios that committed to the headset delivered. The year ended louder than it started.
