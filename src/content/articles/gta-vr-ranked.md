I’ve ranked **every major GTA release** by actual VR playability as of 2026-11-10 — real 6DOF builds first, then limited/wrapper options, then no verified route.

## Ranked GTA VR Options

## #1: Grand Theft Auto: Vice City

![Grand Theft Auto: Vice City](/images/games/grand-theft-auto-vice-city-vr-hero.jpg)

<div class="rank-meta">2002 game · 🟢 Full 6DOF/mod</div>

Vice City is the GTA VR build I’d point a friend at first. It has the widest spread of real routes — a 6DOF/OpenXR mod path, a standalone Quest port path, and UEVR options — so most PCVR/Quest setups have a way in. Setup still takes work and quality varies by hardware, but this is the clearest, most established “stand inside GTA” experience right now.

## #2: Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas

![Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas](/images/games/grand-theft-auto-san-andreas-vr-hero.jpg)

<div class="rank-meta">2004 game · 🟢 Full 6DOF/mod</div>

San Andreas is a real VR conversion, but it asks more of you. The main path is a first-person UEVR conversion of the Definitive Edition with motion controls for driving and shooting — playable, and fiddly to configure. A late-2026 standalone Quest 3 fan build has also been reported (verify provenance/install steps). The official Meta Quest port announced in 2021 remains on indefinite hold. Big, rewarding, not painless.

[Read the full Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas review](/games/grand-theft-auto-san-andreas)

## #3: Grand Theft Auto IV

![Grand Theft Auto IV](/images/games/grand-theft-auto-iv-vr-hero.jpg)

<div class="rank-meta">2008 game · 🟢 Full 6DOF/mod</div>

GTA IV now has a real 6DOF option: **GTA4VR** (by bordplate), a free community mod for *GTA IV: The Complete Edition* on PCVR. It adds tracked head/hand movement, physical weapons, manual interactions, and physical driving through OpenXR — the stuff that actually makes Liberty City feel inhabited. The catch is age of the mod, not ambition: it’s new and actively developed (v0.6.0 as of 2026-11-09), with incomplete mission coverage, bugs, and performance that depends heavily on your rig. If you own GTA IV on PC and don’t mind a work in progress, this is a major upgrade over old injection/wrapper attempts.

[Read the full Grand Theft Auto IV review](/games/grand-theft-auto-iv)

## #4: Grand Theft Auto III

![Grand Theft Auto III](/images/games/gta-3-vr-hero.jpg)

<div class="rank-meta">2001 game · 🟢 Full 6DOF/mod</div>

GTA III now has a proper 6DOF route. The community mod is built on the re3 engine recreation with a native OpenXR VR layer, so head tracking and hand input are first-class: you aim and fire guns with your hands, swing the bat, throw grenades/Molotovs with motion, and drive in first-person with tracked controls and drive-bys. Liberty City’s 2001 open world hits differently when you’re standing inside it. You need a retail PC copy of GTA III, and it’s still a work in progress with control bugs and a manual install — but as a real VR conversion, it clearly belongs in the playable camp.

## #5: Grand Theft Auto V

![Grand Theft Auto V](/images/games/gta-5-vr-hero.jpg)

<div class="rank-meta">2013 game · 🟡 Limited/experimental</div>

GTA V is playable in VR again, but only in a limited way. Luke Ross’s R.E.A.L. VR mod returned with Early Access build 2610.15.2 for *GTA V Enhanced* on 2026-10-15 (hotfix 2610.16.2 on 2026-10-16). It delivers a stereoscopic view in-headset, not motion-controller VR: keyboard/mouse or gamepad only, launch with `-nobattleye`, single-player only, and paid early access. Useful if you want to see Los Santos in a headset; not the same as a full 6DOF conversion.

## Emulator, Wrapper, and Injection Routes

A lot of older GTA titles can be launched in a headset through **emulators, virtual-screen wrappers, or generic injection drivers**. That can be a decent way to revisit them, but it’s still a limited experience compared with a purpose-built 6DOF mod.

- **2D/top-down (GTA, London, GTA 2, Advance):** emulator/wrapper only — virtual screen, no VR presence.
- **PSP (Liberty City Stories, Vice City Stories):** emulator/VR wrapper territory; playable as a flat game in VR.
- **3D-era PC without a verified 6DOF mod:** generic injection drivers or wrappers may work for some setups, but they’re not the same as a dedicated VR conversion.
- **Handheld (Chinatown Wars):** emulator/wrapper only.
- **GTA Online / Trilogy DE:** no verified supported VR route (and R.E.A.L. requires anti-cheat disabled, removing Online).

If a verified, purpose-built VR build appears for any of these, it moves up.

## GTA 6 in VR: The Honest Outlook

GTA VI is dated November 19, 2026, and it’s console-only at launch — PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, with no PC version listed. No VR mode has been announced, and no VR statement of any kind has come from Rockstar or Take-Two. Absence of an announcement isn’t proof it’ll never happen, but it’s the only thing the record supports.

VR remains a long shot: RAGE is a proprietary, closed engine (not an easy UEVR-style target), Rockstar has never shipped a first-party GTA VR mode, the 2021 Meta Quest San Andreas port is still on indefinite hold, and Take-Two DMCA’d Luke Ross’s GTA V/RDR2 mods in 2022. If VR happens, a first-party headset release years after launch is more plausible than day-one VR. For now: flat, on console, at launch.

## The Bottom Line

**Vice City, San Andreas, GTA IV, and GTA III** are the real GTA VR picks right now — all 6DOF/mod routes, with different tradeoffs in maturity and setup. **GTA V** is a limited view-only runtime. Everything else is either an emulator/wrapper-style workaround or has no verified VR route, and **GTA 6** has no VR announced. If you want the best “inside GTA” headset experience today, start with Vice City.
