Pikmin VR finally feels like the thing it always should have been. You're a tiny astronaut commanding an army of two-inch plant-creatures through a backyard that acts like a jungle. Scale is the whole point. Leaning down and seeing your swarm scurry through grass blades taller than they are is exactly the kind of thing a headset should do well.

For years, the only way in was Dolphin VR, a 2016 fork of the GameCube emulator that bolted stereoscopic 3D and head tracking onto the original game. It turned Pikmin into a pretty overhead diorama, then stopped updating. That path is still technically around, but it is frozen in time and not the story anymore.

The story now is **Project Honeydew**, a free native VR mod for the original Pikmin (GameCube) that plays at ground level with your own hands. It is built around first-person scale and hand presence, runs on PC VR and (experimentally) on Quest 3/3S and Steam Frame, and as of version 0.3.1 (released October 28, 2026, per the official site) it is the clear way to play Pikmin in VR. It plays like the thing I assumed was years away.

## How to Play Pikmin VR (Quick Start)

These steps follow the official Project Honeydew (Honeydew 1) install guidance (checked 2026-11-11). Confirm current details on [projecthoneydew.com/honeydew1](https://projecthoneydew.com/honeydew1).

1. **Get a Pikmin disc image you own.** Project Honeydew requires your own Pikmin (GameCube, USA Rev 1) disc image (ISO, GCM, CISO, or NKit). No game files are included.
2. **Download Project Honeydew for your platform.** Choose Windows, Linux, Steam Frame, or the Meta Quest 3/3S build (0.3.1) from the official Honeydew 1 page.
3. **Install and point it at your disc image.** On Windows/Linux, run the launcher, press Install, and select your disc image/folder. On Quest, sideload the APK and copy the disc image (ISO/GCM) to the headset’s Download folder, then select it on first launch for the one-time import.
4. **Start playing.** For PC VR, start SteamVR with your headset connected, then press Play. Without a headset, the PC versions can play on a monitor with a gamepad or keyboard. Quest runs on the headset.
5. **Check platform caveats.** Linux, Steam Frame, and Quest builds are marked experimental/untested in places; Quest voice chat is not available yet in co-op with PCs per the 0.3.1 notes. Treat it as an unofficial fan project and verify current notes on the official site.

**Common issues:** Windows SmartScreen may warn because files are not code-signed (the official site documents the prompt). Quest requires the image as plain ISO/GCM in Download and enough free space for import. If you hit trouble, check the project’s logs and feedback guidance on the official page.

## In the Dirt, Not Above It

Honeydew switches the camera from overhead strategy view to first-person ground level. You are Olimar-sized now, standing in the garden with the Pikmin at your feet. The painted forest wall that normally boxes in the flat version is gone in VR — you see the open horizon beyond the level, which makes the space feel unexpectedly large.

The hands are the center of the design. You reach down and pluck a Pikmin out of the dirt. You grab one by the stem, pull back, and throw it toward a target. A white ring at a Pikmin's feet shows which one your hand is about to grab, which keeps the action readable when the swarm clusters together. Whistling is aimed with your hand, not a cursor. You can pet a Pikmin — it waits under your palm and reacts to each stroke — or punch an enemy with a closed fist if you want Olimar to get personally involved.

Hold the throwing stick up and the view deliberately blinks to roughly eight meters above Olimar, looking down on him like a toy. Let go and it snaps back. A tabletop view sits higher and tilted toward you like a drafting board, where everything looks 1.5× bigger by default and you can zoom by pulling both grips apart. The diorama fantasy is still there; it is just one mode among several, not the whole experience.

The VR UI is thoughtful. A wristwatch shows radar, time of day and a mute badge. Enemy health bars drop to your eye level when they matter. An over-the-shoulder hand camera lets you photograph the squad without your own helmet in frame. Settings are laid out in a headset-friendly menu with tabs, a quick bar, and a render-resolution slider that auto-reverts after ten seconds so you can't accidentally brick your view. It is the kind of polish that suggests someone actually put the headset on while building it.

## Pikmin VR Requirements and Platform Support

Setup is no longer emulator archaeology, but it still has one hard requirement: you need your own Pikmin GameCube disc image, USA Rev 1, as ISO, GCM, CISO or NKit. The mod reads the disc image and never touches or copies the game files. The launcher is straightforward — download, extract/run the launcher, install, point it at your disc image, then hit Play with SteamVR already running for PC VR — but the disc requirement is the gate that decides whether you can play at all.

As of version 0.3.1 (checked 2026-11-11), Windows 64-bit is the main build and the most tested. Linux x86-64 is available for Linux PCs and Steam Deck (playing flat on Deck), but the project notes it as untested on real hardware. There is an experimental Steam Frame ARM64 build and an experimental Meta Quest 3 / 3S standalone APK via SideQuest or `adb install`. The Quest build needs the disc image copied over USB as .iso or .gcm and performs a one-time import. Online play works across Windows, Linux, Steam Frame, and Quest on the same version, with voice chat not yet available on Quest per the 0.3.1 notes. The app is unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen will complain until you click through. The site publishes SHA-256 checksums and VirusTotal scan links for releases.

Headset support is honest about its limits. The mod was built and tested on the original HTC Vive and tested on Meta Quest 3 through Link. Valve Index, Steam Frame streaming, Quest via Steam Link / Air Link / Virtual Desktop, and other SteamVR headsets are described as untested. If you are on a headset outside the tested pair, you are in early-adopter territory.

## Pikmin VR Controls and What Works Well

The physical scale works. Pikmin feel appropriately tiny, Bulborbs feel appropriately massive, and the spatial relationships that matter in combat and routing are easier to read when you are standing inside them rather than hovering above them. The motion controls map cleanly to the game's actions, and the quality-of-life touches add up: you can pluck, throw, whistle, pet, and manage the swarm in ways that make sense in first person, with options like tabletop/overhead views when you want them.

Online co-op is built for two, mixed VR and monitor play, with features such as proximity voice chat, high-fives, and handing Pikmin between players (per project notes). Room codes keep joining simple, and both players need the same version; Windows, Linux, Steam Frame, and Quest builds of the same version can play together, with voice chat not yet on Quest as of 0.3.1. Monitor play is also supported if you do not want to stay in the headset the whole time.

But the project is still young in the ways that matter to trust. Independent coverage is thin, and some platform paths remain experimental or lightly tested. That is not a knock on the build quality; it is a reason to keep expectations calibrated.

Performance is generally modest for a 2001 GameCube game. The optional Pikmin 4K texture pack by Henriko Magnifico adds VRAM load and can stutter briefly the first time an area's textures load, which is normal for a big pack. Base play is comfortable, seated mode and snap turn are supported, and the site is upfront that VR can cause motion discomfort in general.

## Pikmin VR vs Pikmin 2 VR and Other Versions

This is the original Pikmin — not [Pikmin 2 in VR](/games/pikmin-2/), not Pikmin 3 Deluxe, not Pikmin 4, not the Switch remasters, not Hey! Pikmin on 3DS. Project Honeydew needs exactly that GameCube USA Rev 1 disc. If you do not own it, you cannot play. Pikmin 2 is its own product with its own guide (including Project Honeydew 2 and the legacy Dolphin VR route), and the broader franchise’s Switch versions remain the definitive flat way to play. If your question is “how to play Pikmin in VR,” this original Pikmin page is the one you want; if it’s “how to play Pikmin 2 in VR,” head to the Pikmin 2 guide.

## Is Pikmin VR Worth Playing?

The thing the old emulator path only hinted at — standing in the garden, hands in the dirt, actually directing a swarm at real scale — now exists and works. Project Honeydew is a real native VR mod with real interaction design, real co-op options, and a real respect for the original game. It is also unofficial, requires your own disc image, and still has experimental or lightly tested corners depending on platform.

If you own the disc and have a supported PC VR setup (or want to try Quest 3/3S standalone with eyes open), this is worth the moderate setup. If you do not own the disc, the question is moot until you source one. The garden is finally worth visiting in a headset. Just know you are visiting a fan project, not a Nintendo release.
