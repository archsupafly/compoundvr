Pikmin 2 VR finally has the version people imagine: you stand at captain height, reach down into the dirt, and send a swarm into the grass with your own hands. **Project Honeydew 2** is a free, unofficial VR mod for Pikmin 2 (GameCube) that puts the caves, treasures, and backyard panic right at eye level on PC VR, Steam Frame, and Meta Quest 3/3S. It is the place to start if you want to play Pikmin 2 in VR today.

The older path — Dolphin VR with a first-person camera mod — still exists, and its sense of scale is still special. But it is a 2016 emulator fork with gamepad controls and a lot of setup friction. If you came here for that legacy route, it is covered below.

## Pikmin 2 VR with Project Honeydew 2

Project Honeydew 2 is a hands-on, native VR take on Pikmin 2, not a stereoscopic overlay. According to the official project page (checked 2026-11-11), you play as Captain Olimar and Louie at their size: you pluck sprouts from the ground, grab Pikmin, and throw them with a real swing. The same tiny-world magic that always defined Pikmin 2 — a Bulborb that feels couch-sized, treasure hunts in spaces that look enormous from an inch off the soil — is the whole point, only now your hands are in the loop.

This first release is labeled **version 0.1.0 preview**. The project lists Windows, Linux, Steam Frame, and Meta Quest 3/3S builds, with Windows as the main version and the Linux, Steam Frame, and Quest builds described as new and experimental. On PC you can play in VR through SteamVR or on a monitor without a headset. The Quest build runs on the headset itself. Features described by the project include wrist tools (watch/radar), two-captain/cave play, tabletop viewing, comfort options, and a friend-on-the-monitor co-op mode where a gamepad player controls the other captain while you play in VR. As with any preview, expect rough edges and check the official page for the current state.

Because it is an unofficial fan mod, you bring your own copy of Pikmin 2. No game files are included. That keeps the setup grounded in a real requirement: if you do not have a compatible GameCube disc image, there is nothing to play.

## How to Play Pikmin 2 VR (Quick Start)

These steps follow the official Project Honeydew 2 install guidance (checked 2026-11-11). Always confirm current details on [projecthoneydew.com](https://projecthoneydew.com/).

1. **Get a Pikmin 2 disc image you own.** The project states PC/Steam Frame use a USA (GPVE01) GameCube image (ISO, GCM, CISO, or NKit; RVZ/WIA/GCZ with dolphin-tool). The Quest build lists USA or Europe as a plain ISO/GCM. No game files are provided by the mod.
2. **Download Project Honeydew 2 for your platform.** Grab the Windows, Linux, Steam Frame, or Meta Quest 3/3S build (0.1.0 preview) from the official site.
3. **Install and point it at your disc image.** On PC/Steam Frame, run the launcher/installer and select your disc image. On Quest, sideload the APK and copy the disc image to the headset’s Download folder, then select it on first launch for the one-time import.
4. **Start in VR (or on a monitor).** For PC VR, start SteamVR with your headset connected, then press Play. Quest runs on the headset. The PC versions can also run on a monitor without VR.
5. **Check platform caveats.** The Linux, Steam Frame, and Quest builds are marked experimental in this preview, and the project asks players to report issues. Read the current notes before you assume feature parity.

**Common issues:** Windows may show a SmartScreen warning for unsigned files (the official site documents this). The Quest build requires a plain ISO/GCM on the headset and sideloading. If something feels off, treat it as preview software and verify against the project’s latest install/feedback notes.

## Dolphin VR for Pikmin 2 (Legacy Route)

Before Honeydew 2, the only practical way to play Pikmin 2 in VR was **Dolphin VR**, an old fork of the GameCube/Wii emulator with stereoscopic 3D and head tracking. It is still a way in, but it is a legacy option: the last public build is **Dolphin VR 5.0-250** (July 2016), and the project has been effectively abandoned. You play with a gamepad, not motion controllers.

There is a moment in Pikmin 2 VR on Dolphin where you stand up, look down, and realize the Bulborb snoring two feet in front of you is the size of a couch. The grass blades are like palm trees. A single Red Pikmin at your feet comes up to your knee. That scale shift is the whole pitch, even here: you're not watching Captain Olimar from above anymore. You *are* the tiny astronaut, lost in somebody's overgrown backyard, throwing a hundred living vegetables at monsters the size of sedans.

The catch? You don't actually throw them.

For the best Dolphin VR experience, you want the first-person camera mod. Unlike the original Pikmin, Pikmin 2 tolerates a low camera much better. With the right AR/Gecko codes and a bit of file editing, you can drop the viewpoint to Olimar's eye level and play the campaign from the ground. Getting there is not a quick afternoon project: you need a legally obtained GameCube ISO, both Dolphin VR and a recent mainline Dolphin for configuration, culling-disable codes, camera codes, and patience. This is advanced-setup territory.

Once it works, the size still lands. The stereoscopic depth helps with judging distances, and the head-tracked camera lets you glance around to manage squads. But the interaction loop stays flat: gamepad buttons for pluck/throw/swarm, no hand presence, GameCube UI in your face. Comfort is mixed in smooth first-person, and the old toolchain can mean flicker, audio hitches, or crashes.

## Pikmin 2 VR: Honeydew 2 vs Dolphin VR

If you want Pikmin 2 VR with motion controls, **Project Honeydew 2** is the answer: hands-on interaction, modern platform targets (including Quest 3/3S), and active development, with the honest caveat that it is an early preview. If you specifically want the emulator-tinkering project — or you enjoy making old tools behave — **Dolphin VR** still offers that first-person garden view on PCVR, but with a gamepad, abandoned software, and real setup friction.

For most people asking how to play Pikmin 2 in VR now, Honeydew 2 is the better starting point.

## The Good Parts

Pikmin 2 is a perfect fit for VR scale. It is one of Nintendo's best GameCube games — longer than the original, with caves, no day limit, dozens of treasures, and two-player challenge mode — and seeing its world at life size gives it a presence the flat version never had. Honeydew 2 finally adds the missing piece: reaching in and handling Pikmin with your hands. Even on the legacy Dolphin route, that ground-level garden perspective can still feel special when it all comes together.

## The Caveats

Project Honeydew 2 is a preview: builds on some platforms are experimental, behavior can change, and you should expect rough edges. It is also unofficial and requires your own GameCube disc image. Dolphin VR, meanwhile, is a 2016 emulator fork that nobody actively maintains, with gamepad-only input and advanced setup. Neither path is a polished, official Nintendo VR release — but Honeydew 2 is the one that feels like a modern answer to "Pikmin 2 in VR."

## Who This Is Really For

If you own Pikmin 2 and want the closest thing to a real Pikmin 2 VR experience, start with Project Honeydew 2 and accept that you are riding an early preview. If you are a VR tinkerer who likes the archaeology of old emulator builds, Dolphin VR is still an interesting weekend project. If you just want to play Pikmin 2 with zero fuss, the flat version remains the cleanest way in. For everyone else chasing that tiny-astronaut-in-the-garden feeling in a headset, Honeydew 2 is finally the route worth trying.
